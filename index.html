Chandrajyoti Construction and Suppliers Pvt. Ltd. is a government-licensed construction company based in Kathmandu, Nepal. Registered under the Office of the Company Registrar (Reg. No. 140208/072/073) and licensed by the Ministry of Physical Infrastructure and Transport, we have built a trusted track record delivering government and private contract projects across Nepal.

Specializing in building construction, road networks, bridge construction, and government contract works, we combine skilled engineering with disciplined project execution. Every project is delivered with an uncompromised commitment to quality craftsmanship, safety compliance, and client satisfaction.
